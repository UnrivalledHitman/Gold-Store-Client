| :--- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | :---: |

## 7. Manager Pitch-Call Objection Handling Scripts

### Objection 1: *"Another agency quoted ₹2.5L–₹4L on Shopify / WordPress. Why is yours ₹14L?"*
> **Response:** "Shopify or WooCommerce works great for ₹1,500 t-shirts, and we could build a basic template site for ₹3L too—but for fine gold jewellery, templates break fast. You end up paying recurring SaaS fees plus `0.5%–2%` platform commissions on high-value ₹1L–₹5L orders, patching fragile plugins for 22KT/18KT gram-weight pricing, `≥ ₹2L` PAN KYC compliance, and `3%` GST breakup invoices. Our ₹14L package gives you **full IP ownership with zero per-order SaaS fees**, an already-built luxury Next.js engine, **100-SKU macro photography + copywriting**, and **SEO/CAPI + Month-1 ads** under one roof—saving you more in commissions alone in Year 1 than the price difference."

### Objection 2: *"Can we compress the timeline from 75 days to 45 days?"*
> **Response:** "Because we already have the PIXTACY frontend architecture ready, we can actually demo your branded UI very early—within 3–4 weeks. What takes 75 days isn't just coding; it's **Razorpay & Sequel Logistics jewellery KYC approvals (which take 2–3 weeks externally), studio shooting & retouching 100 reflective gold SKUs cleanly, PAN compliance testing, and Google Merchant Center verification**. If we rush those external gates, launch breaks on Day 1. At 75 days, we launch cleanly—and Month-1 ad management runs for a full 30 days *after* Go-Live so you don't lose a single day of marketing."

### Objection 3: *"Why isn't Google/Meta ad spend included in the ₹1,80,000 marketing fee?"*
> **Response:** "Keeping media spend direct to Google and Meta is actually to protect *your* transparency and control. Your ad accounts stay 100% in `[Client Brand Name]`'s name and GST billing—we take zero hidden markup on your ad budget, and you can dial media spend up during Akshaya Tritiya or wedding season or pause anytime without agency lock-in. Our fee covers the specialist engineering (SEO, 4 showroom GBPs, GA4 + Meta CAPI server tracking, Merchant Center feeds, ad creatives) plus 30 days of active post-launch optimization."

### Objection 4: *"We have 300–350 SKUs ready to launch. Can you absorb all of them in the same ₹14L price?"*
> **Response:** "That's why we structured Creative as a **100-SKU baseline allowance (`₹1,20,000`) with a discounted Per-SKU Rate Card** instead of guessing a rigid number. For 300 SKUs, `101–250` drops to `₹1,100/SKU` and `251+` drops to `₹950/SKU`—or if you already have clean studio photos for half your catalog, we upload those at just `₹250/SKU`. Even better, we launch on **Day 75** with your top 100–120 hero SKUs live and generating revenue, then batch-load the remaining SKUs in Weeks 11–13 so inventory volume never delays your store launch."
