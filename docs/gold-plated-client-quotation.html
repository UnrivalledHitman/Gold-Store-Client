01 · Strategic Overview

Built Specifically for High-Conversion Gold-Plated Jewellery

[Client Brand Name] is transforming an established traditional gold-plated jewellery retail business into a modern, pan-India D2C and showroom-integrated brand. Winning online in gold-plated and demi-fine jewellery requires sub-second mobile speed for Instagram/Meta ad traffic, transparent base-metal & plating care details, bridal/combo bundles that lift Average Order Value (AOV), seamless WhatsApp real-light video enquiries, and disciplined Cash-on-Delivery (COD) RTO control.

Our team already has a working Next.js storefront prototype in our repository showcasing editorial luxury merchandising, multi-facet catalog filtering, interactive Shop the Look hotspots, automatic bundle discounts, and omnichannel showroom/WhatsApp workflows.

Faster Mobile Ad ROAS

Next.js App Router + Tailwind CSS on Vercel loads dramatically faster on budget mobile devices than script-heavy template themes, protecting Meta & Google Ad conversion rates.

Zero Recurring USD App Bloat

On ₹1,200–₹3,500 AOV orders, SaaS subscriptions plus paid apps (bundles, COD OTP, Shiprocket, swatches, WhatsApp) erode margins. Custom Next.js + Supabase keeps it all native.

100% Modular & Client-Owned

Toggle optional Branding, 100-SKU Photo/Video, SEO/Ads, or PM blocks freely. Upon Milestone 4 settlement, [Client Brand Name] owns 100% of the code and database.
