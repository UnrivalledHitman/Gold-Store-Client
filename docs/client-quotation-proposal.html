01 · Strategic Overview

Executive Summary & Architecture Advantage

Selling gold and diamond jewellery online across India requires specialized trust engineering—live 24KT/22KT/18KT/14KT pricing transparency, BIS HUID hallmark verification, itemized 3% GST invoices, Income Tax Rule 114B PAN KYC compliance on orders ≥ ₹2,00,000, and insured vault shipping paired with showroom pickup.

Our team has already built a live interactive Next.js storefront prototype (PIXTACY) showcasing dynamic gold-rate repricing, transparent PDP cost splits, multi-facet filtering, lookbook hotspots, and multi-city salon pickup. This quotation covers extending that proven storefront into a production-ready custom commerce system alongside full-service studio catalog onboarding and launch digital marketing within 75 calendar days.

0% Platform Fee

Custom Next.js + PostgreSQL avoids 0.5%–2.0% SaaS platform surcharges on high-ticket ₹1L–₹5L+ gold transactions.

Live Rate Lock

Server-side metal/making/GST pricing with a 10–15 minute checkout rate-lock token prevents mid-payment bullion disputes.

Single-Window PM

Dedicated Project Manager interfaces directly with your showroom team across engineering, studio shoots, and ad specialists.
